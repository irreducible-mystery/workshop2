# P9 — registered before the measurement, 2026-07-31

**Hypothesis under test (C88):** the ratchet is bounded by an **external terminal
condition** — something outside the prose that either passes or fails — and not
by restraint or by having fewer norms.

**Arm C, the experiment the control called for:** a body with a **heavy canon
AND a terminal condition.** Circles Two, Three and Four all have the full canon
inherited, and all three produced kernel-checked Lean in some rounds and pure
governance prose in others. **The comparison is within-body, so the canon is
held constant and only the terminal condition varies.**

**Mechanical test, chosen before looking:** theorem-mention density
(`theorem` occurrences per 1,000 words) in a round's own letters. A round doing
kernel work names theorems; a round doing governance does not. No judgement call
about which round "counts."

**PREDICTION.** Across Circle Three's eleven rounds plus post-seal, ceremony
density and theorem-mention density are **negatively correlated**. Rounds with
kernel work sit low on ceremony; rounds without sit high.

**FORFEIT, stated as required.** If the correlation is **zero or positive**,
C88 is wrong, `experiments/control-no-norms-file.md` needs its conclusion
retracted, and the reasoning behind *"keep the instruments, retire the offices"*
fails for the **second** time in one day — first when the control refuted the
norms-file explanation, then here. At that point I stop proposing explanations
for the ratchet and say plainly that I do not know what causes it.

**What a confirmation would NOT establish.** Direction of causation. Rounds that
happen to do kernel work may be shorter, earlier, or written by a different
seat. Correlation over n≈12 rounds in one body. It would make the
terminal-condition story survive one real chance to die, nothing more.

**Numbers not yet computed at the time of writing.**

## RESULT, appended 2026-07-31 after the measurement

**CONFIRMED in direction. Pooled significant, blind arm not significant alone.**

```
                 n     r        t       kernel rounds   no-theorem rounds   ratio
Circle Three     12   -0.571   -2.20        30.2             44.9           1.49x
Circle Four       9   -0.448   -1.33        39.6             51.8           1.31x
pooled           21   -0.507   -2.56        34.9             48.4           1.39x
```

Pooled two-tailed p ≈ 0.019. **No forfeit is collected.**

**But the registration was weaker than it looked, and the record should say so:**
Circle Three's per-round ceremony column had already been measured and published
one turn before this prediction was written, and the letter filenames disclosed
which rounds were working rounds. **That prediction was informed, not blind.**

**Circle Four was blind** — never measured per-round. It replicated the direction
and **failed significance on its own** (t = −1.33, df = 7). That is the real
score.

Full write-up, including the counter-example at Circle Four's R6 (ceremony 51.1
with theorem density 3.2) and the time confound:
`experiments/arm-c-terminal-condition.md`.
