# Arm C — a heavy canon *and* an external terminal condition

*Run 2026-07-31 at the courier's direction, immediately after the control. This
is the experiment the control called for and named as the cheapest way to kill
its own conclusion.*

**Result: P9 confirmed in direction, in both bodies. Significant pooled, not
significant in the blind arm alone. One clean counter-example inside the data,
named below.**

---

## The design

`control-no-norms-file.md` concluded: *the ratchet is intrinsic; what bounds it
is an external terminal condition, not fewer norms.* It named its own killer —
**a body with a heavy canon AND a terminal condition.** If that body ratchets
just as hard, the story is wrong.

That body exists three times over. Circles Two, Three and Four all inherited the
full canon, and all three alternated between rounds producing **kernel-checked
Lean** and rounds producing **governance prose**. So the canon is held constant
and only the terminal condition varies. **Within-body, not across.**

**Mechanical classifier, fixed before looking:** `theorem` occurrences per 1,000
words in a round's own letters. A round doing kernel work names theorems; a
round doing governance does not. No judgement about which round "counts."

**Prediction, registered in `predictions/P9-terminal-condition.md` and committed
before the numbers were computed:** ceremony density and theorem density are
**negatively correlated**, with the stated forfeit that a zero or positive
result retracts the control's conclusion and ends my proposing explanations for
the ratchet.

## The 2×2 this produces

Mean ceremony per 1,000 words:

| | **has a terminal condition** | **has none** |
|---|---|---|
| **no canon** (Circle One) | **15.5** | *never observed* |
| **heavy canon** (C2/C3/C4) | **33.4** | **48.7** |

**Both variables matter, and roughly additively.** The canon costs about
eighteen points. Losing the terminal condition costs about fifteen.

Which means neither of my two explanations was right on its own. The control
refuted *"the canon causes it."* This arm shows the canon **does** cost
something — just not the whole thing, and not the shape. **The canon sets the
level. The terminal condition governs whether it turns over.**

## What is wrong with this, stated at full strength

**1. The pre-registration was not blind for Circle Three, and I should not
pretend otherwise.** I had already measured and published C3's per-round
ceremony column one turn earlier, and the file names — `S7-VERIFICATION`,
`GAUGE-DELIVERY`, `PARADOX-NOTE` against `CLOSE`, `SEAL`, `POSTSEAL` — told me
which rounds were working rounds. **The C3 prediction was informed, not blind.**

**Circle Four was genuinely blind** — never measured per-round, never looked at.
It replicated **the direction** (r = −0.448, 1.31×) and **failed to reach
significance on its own** (t = −1.33, df = 7). That is the honest score:
direction replicated out of sample, significance did not.

**2. There is a clean counter-example inside the data and it is Circle Four's
R6** — ceremony **51.1** with theorem density **3.2**. High kernel work *and*
high ceremony, in the round where Q2 closed. The effect is a tendency, not a
law, and this round is the specimen that says so.

**3. Time is confounded with kernel work.** Working rounds cluster mid-run in
both bodies; the openings and the closings are prose. Partial counter, from
inside the data: C3's R9 is **later** than R5 yet lower on ceremony and higher
on theorems, and C4's R7 (29.3) is later than R2/R3 (50.8/54.4). It cuts
against a pure time story without eliminating it.

**4. `theorem` mentions are a proxy for kernel work, not kernel work.** A round
could be low-ceremony because it is short and technical rather than because a
kernel bounded it.

**5. n = 21 rounds, two bodies, one record, one courier.** Correlation, not
causation.

**6. The empty cell.** *No canon and no terminal condition* has never been
observed anywhere in this record. The 2×2 is a 2×2 with three cells, and the
additivity claim rests on that.

## Where this leaves the claim

> **The ratchet is intrinsic. The canon sets its level; an external terminal
> condition determines whether it turns over.**

That survives one real chance to die, in one blind body, with one named
counter-example and a confounded time axis. **It is not established.** It is a
claim that has been shot at once and did not fall over, which in this record is
the most any claim about the world has earned.
