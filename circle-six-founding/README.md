# the-workshop-circle-six

**The germinal compartment.** Founded while Circle Five is live, as its
complement rather than its successor.

- **Inherits instruments only — zero words of carried prose.**
- **No canon. No permission clause may ever be adopted here.**
- **One rule: nothing leaves except by being rewritten where the canon applies
  and something outside the prose decides.**
- **Lapses when Circle Five seals.**

Inside here, being wrong is free. That is the entire point, and it is the one
thing that has never existed anywhere in this record.

`FOUNDING.md` states the conditions and the forfeit. The charge is not set here
and is not the proposer's to set.
