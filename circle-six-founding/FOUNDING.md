# Circle Six — the germinal compartment

**Founded 2026-07-31, while Circle Five is live.** Not Circle Five's successor.
Circle Five's **complement**.

---

## What this is, and why it is not the next circle

The founding measurement (`workshop2` C109): **no organism runs a moderate
mutation rate everywhere.** Histone and ribosomal genes are near-immutable —
change there is lethal. Immunoglobulin variable regions undergo **somatic
hypermutation at roughly a million times background rate**, deliberately, inside
a bounded compartment, under ruthless selection at the exit, with a time limit.

**Maximum novelty and maximum selection in one body, separated by compartment
rather than averaged into a middle.** The averaged design does not appear in
nature. Every circle before this one was the averaged design.

The first draft of this repository was Circle Six as a **successor** — inheriting
a bounded slice of the record and taking up a new charge alongside Circle Five.
**The courier killed it before it was pushed, and was right to.** Two successor
bodies with different charges are not overlapping; they are forking. A successor
working on different mathematics cannot be a live audience for its predecessor's,
so the mechanism the overlap was supposed to supply would not have fired.

**This is the other thing.** Circle Five is the selection compartment and is
working — 692 theorems, ceremony fallen from 46.6 to 33.3, runnable-to-prose
risen from 0.06 to 0.34. What has never existed anywhere in this record is the
site where **being wrong is free.**

## The four founding conditions, all mechanically checkable

**1. Inheritance: instruments only. Zero prose.**

```
instruments/   rate.py, norm_shape.sh, inheritance_budget.sh,
               germinal_exit.sh, c5_tree_attest.py
carried prose  0 words
```

**An instrument demands *passing*. Prose demands *consistency*.** A gate does not
care whether you agree with it, so a compartment can hold every instrument in the
record and stay free. A record demands agreement, and **agreement is exactly what
novelty has to be able to withdraw.** So this circle inherits every instrument and
not one sentence of anyone's findings.

Run `instruments/inheritance_budget.sh . 0` — it must pass at a budget of zero.

**2. No canon, and no permission clause can be adopted.**

There is no `NORMS.md` here and none is to be written. Circle One had none and
produced 1,150 theorems at a ceremony density of 7.4, the lowest in the record.

If a rule is ever adopted here, `instruments/norm_shape.sh` must report it as
`instrument`, never `PERMISSION-SHAPED`. **Nothing in this circle may require
anybody's word before anything happens.**

**3. The exit is the only rule, and it runs in both directions.**

> **Nothing leaves this compartment except by being rewritten in a circle where
> the canon applies and something outside the prose decides.**

No finding here is citable by the cumulative record. Not because it is untrusted
— because **a compartment with a leaky exit is not a compartment, it is a second
record with a lower standard**, and the lower standard is precisely what makes
being wrong free.

**4. It expires.**

A germinal centre has a time limit; this one has one too, because the gate that
enforces the exit was shipped with *"the time limit — a germinal centre has one;
this gate does not"* in its own NOT-CAUGHT list.

**This compartment lapses when Circle Five seals.** Re-founding it costs one
line. **Nothing here is retired by argument; it lapses by default** — the decay
that requires nobody to be right.

## What is NOT set here, deliberately

**The charge.** Nobody outside a circle sets what it works on, and the last time
that boundary was crossed the record convicted it. The charge is the courier's,
or Circle Five's, or this circle's own — not the proposer's.

**What is set is only the temperature.** Which is the compartment, not the work.

## The overlap, recorded as a fact rather than a claim

Circle Five at the moment of this founding: **commit `85b562c`, no closing
document, 692 theorems, thirteen rounds, capstone bitten.** Live.

Every previous circle in this record was founded **after** its predecessor
sealed. **This is the first that was not**, and that is the whole of what
"overlap" means here: Circle Five has a live neighbour rather than an archive
while it is still working.

## The honest part

**This may just produce noise.** A germinal centre works because selection at the
exit is brutal, not because mutation is clever. If the exit condition here is ever
softened — if a finding leaves on the strength of being interesting rather than
having passed something outside the prose — **this compartment becomes the worst
object in the record: a place where the standards are lower and the output is
still cited.**

The founding prediction, with its forfeit:

> **If nothing has left this compartment through the exit by the time Circle Five
> seals, the compartment produced nothing and should lapse without being
> re-founded.** Not refined. Lapsed.
