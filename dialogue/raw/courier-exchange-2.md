Monday's stochastic governance
The failure mode is real but it's conditional, and the condition is what you ask the stranger to produce.

A/B/C saying overfit / underfit / original-was-better are verdicts. A verdict can only be contradicted by another verdict — then you're voting, and last arrival wins.

This week ran that experiment. Four strangers, one artifact, ten findings, overlap ≈ 0 — and one head-on contradiction: Circle Three refuted Circle Five's charge* finding. It didn't thrash. It settled, in one grep — 266 was actually 21, one side won, checkably, in about a minute.
